### Research Topics

The positions are funded by the [ERC project OPTiMiSE](/erc/).
The research focuses on evolution problems from a variational and geometric
perspective, with particular emphasis on metric structures, gradient flows,
dissipative processes, and the evolution of measures. Key areas include:

- Evolution problems, gradient flows, and quasistatic dynamics of critical points in metric spaces and in the space of probability measures
- Variational structures and dissipation principles for PDEs
- Variational approximation methods, iterative schemes, and optimal control approaches
- Optimal transport, in both its classical and entropic (Schrödinger problem) formulations
- Connections with optimization models and applications to Machine Learning and generative models

The specific research program will be defined in agreement with the selected
candidates, and may also extend beyond the areas listed above, provided it is
consistent with the objectives of the ERC project.

### Requirements

Candidates should hold a PhD or an equivalent qualification, obtained in Italy or
abroad, or be in the process of completing their PhD within six months from the
publication date of the call. A strong background in analysis, PDEs, or optimal
transport is an advantage.
