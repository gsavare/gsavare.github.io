### Research Topics

The position is funded by the [ERC project OPTiMiSE](/erc/).
The research focuses on the analysis of metric structures, gradient flows,
dissipative processes, and the evolution of measures. Key areas include:

- Optimal transport
- Variational analysis of PDEs
- Optimization models
- Potential applications to Machine Learning

### Requirements

Candidates should have (or be close to completing) a PhD in Mathematics or a related field, with a strong background in analysis, PDEs, or optimal transport.
