**Dates:** November 23–27, 2026<br>
**Location:** Aula Dini, Palazzo del Castelletto, Pisa (Italy)<br>
**Website:** [crmdegiorgi.sns.it/evento/optimal-transportation-and-applications](https://crmdegiorgi.sns.it/evento/optimal-transportation-and-applications/)

The 12th edition of the traditional Pisa meeting on Optimal Transport, started in 2001. The workshop collects the latest contributions from leading researchers in the field and its applications, providing a comprehensive overview of the remarkable developments in several theoretical and new applied directions.
